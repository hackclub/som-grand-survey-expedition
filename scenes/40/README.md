# Scene 40: Isle of the Riddle Second

A moonlit beach with ancient ruins containing a runic puzzle that must be solved to progress.

## Puzzle Solution

### Runic Puzzle

The puzzle presents four runes with the following values:
- ᚢ = 5
- ᚠ = 10
- ᚨ = 15
- ᚱ = 20

To solve the puzzle, click the runes in ascending order:

**Solution: ᚢ → ᚠ → ᚨ → ᚱ**

(Click the runes with values: 5 → 10 → 15 → 20)

This sequence will activate the seal and reveal the teleport to the next scene.

### Seal Puzzle

After solving the runic puzzle, you'll encounter a 15-puzzle (sliding tile puzzle) that must be solved to break the seal on the ancient tomb. Arrange the tiles to complete the image of the seal.

## How to Play

1. Explore the beach scene by clicking on various elements
2. Click on the tablet to activate the runic puzzle
3. Solve the runic puzzle by clicking runes in the correct order
4. Complete the seal puzzle to unlock the tomb
5. Enter the tomb to discover the treasure chamber
